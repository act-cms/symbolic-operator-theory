Exploration of Quantum Mechanical Operators in SymPy
====================================================

This module provides a set of lessons which are designed to introduce physical
chemistry students to the mathematical properties of quantum mechanical
operators and the cyberinfrastructure skills necessary to encode, manipulate,
and evaluate operator expressions in Python using the SymPy library.

## Lesson/Module Contents

Different physical chemistry courses with different student populations,
different course learning outcomes, and different math course prerequisites
will all interact with quantum mechanical operators at a different level of
conceptual depth and mathematical rigor. To accommodate these differences, this
module provides the following lesson versions which treat this topic
differently: 

1. Level 1: Essentials of QM Operators (coming soon!)
    * Directory: `01_essentials`
    * Description: Introduces operators entirely within the context of
    1-dimensional model systems, focuses on practical CI skills rather than
    mathematical depth
    * Intended courses: 
        - Biophysical chemistry,
        - 1-semester survey of physical chemistry, or
        - Any physical chemistry course with prereq of only 1 semester of calculus
    * Time: 2-3 hours
2. Level 2: Intermediate QM Operators (coming soon!)
    * Directory: `02_intermediate`
    * Description: Introduces operators within the context of 1-dimensional model
    systems, however expands discussion to include properties of eigenstates
    * Intended courses: 
        - 2-semester undergraduate physical chemistry sequence, or
        - Any physical chemistry course with prereq of 2 semesters of calculus
    * Time: 3 hours
3. Level 3: Advanced QM Operators
    * Directory: `03_advanced`
    * Description: Introduces operators as mathematical entities and explores
    properties of operators and eigenstates in detail before introducing quantum
    mechanical applications.
    * Intended courses: 
        - 2-semester honors undergraduate physical chemistry,
        - first-year graduate quantum mechanics (taught in Chemistry department,
        not from Physics!)
        - Any physical chemistry course with prereq of 3 semesters of calculus
        or 2 semesters of calculus + linear algebra/discrete mathematics
    * Time: 6 hours
